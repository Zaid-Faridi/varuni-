# Watershed Geospatial Intelligence Platform - Master Blueprint

Title: Application of Geospatial Techniques for Visualization and Analysis to Interpret Geo-Coded Images to Enhance Watershed Development Outcomes
Organization: Ministry of Rural Development

## Four-part structure
1. 01_ARCHITECTURE - A-to-Z engineering and product architecture, data flow, modules, APIs, infrastructure, observability, deployment, and micro-interactions.
2. 02_UI_UX - Complete product experience, information architecture, screens, components, states, accessibility, responsive behavior, motion, and design system.
3. 03_AI - Every proposed AI/ML/CV/remote-sensing technique, model, algorithm, tool, evaluation method, and fallback strategy.
4. 04_RULES - Non-negotiable engineering, security, privacy, geospatial, government, data-governance, AI, accessibility, and operational rules.

## Source boundary
The source problem statement requires an integrated GIS/remote-sensing framework, geo-coded image interpretation, thematic mapping, watershed monitoring, evidence-based decision support, scalability/cost effectiveness, and stronger use of SRISHTI-DRISHTI 30 m satellite data. It does not prescribe a specific software stack, AI model, UI, or database. Those choices in this blueprint are engineering recommendations and must be validated against the actual data/API access supplied by the sponsoring authority.
